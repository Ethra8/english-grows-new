{% trans "3. Why do we process your personal data?" %}

{% trans "Individual training and account management" %}

{% blocktrans %} We process the information necessary to manage accounts, organise courses, arrange lessons, deliver training and communicate with learners about the services they have contracted. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% trans "Performance of a contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR." %}

{% trans "Company-funded training" %}

{% blocktrans %} Where an employer contracts English Grows, we process information necessary to organise participation, deliver training, assess learning and maintain relevant training records. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% blocktrans %} Legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR in organising and delivering agreed corporate training and supporting learners' academic development. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Academic assessment and Learning Needs" %}

{% blocktrans %} Academic information is processed to identify learning requirements, personalise teaching, assess language development and support training delivery. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% blocktrans %} Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for individually contracted training, or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for company-funded training, as applicable. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Placement tests" %}

{% blocktrans %} We process submitted answers and assessment information to calculate a recommended course level and communicate the result to the participant. Where a teacher is assigned, a separate result notification is sent to that teacher; otherwise, authorised English Grows administration receives the result for placement administration. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% blocktrans %} Legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR in providing the requested assessment and supporting appropriate course placement. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Optional marketing communications" %}

{% blocktrans %} Where you actively subscribe, we use your email address to send English Grows news, offers and information about our training services. Subscription is optional and requires an affirmative action through a checkbox that is not preselected. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% blocktrans %} Consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, together with the applicable requirements of Article 21 LSSI-CE. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} Completing a placement test or creating an account does not automatically subscribe you to marketing. You may withdraw consent at any time using the unsubscribe mechanism in our marketing emails or by contacting us. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Enquiries and customer support" %}

{% blocktrans %} We process information provided in enquiries to respond to requests and manage communications concerning our services. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% blocktrans %} Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where necessary for steps preceding a contract, or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for general enquiries and customer support. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Billing and payment administration" %}

{% blocktrans %} We process billing and transaction information to administer payments, issue invoices and maintain accounting records. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% blocktrans %} Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where necessary for the contracted service and Article 6(1)(c) GDPR for applicable accounting and tax obligations. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Platform operation and security" %}

{% blocktrans %} Necessary technical information is processed to maintain the website, protect accounts, investigate technical incidents and prevent unauthorised access. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% blocktrans %} Legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR in maintaining a secure and functional service, or Article 6(1)(c) GDPR where a specific legal obligation applies. {% endblocktrans %}
