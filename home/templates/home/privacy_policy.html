{% trans "4. Why do we process your personal data?" %}

{% blocktrans %} We process personal information for defined purposes. The applicable legal basis depends on the activity and your relationship with English Grows. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Individual training and account management" %}

{% blocktrans %} We process the information necessary to manage your account, organise Courses, arrange lessons and deliver training contracted directly with English Grows. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% blocktrans trimmed %} Performance of a contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, where the processing is necessary for that contract. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Company-funded training" %}

{% blocktrans %} Where your employer contracts English Grows to provide training, we process information necessary to organise your participation, deliver lessons, assess learning and maintain relevant training records. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} Your employer is responsible for selecting participants and providing their necessary information. English Grows acts as an independent controller for its own training delivery and academic activities. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% blocktrans trimmed %} Legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, in organising and delivering the agreed corporate training and supporting learners' academic development. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} The information shared with corporate administrators is limited to what is necessary for their training administration and reporting responsibilities. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Academic assessment and Learning Needs" %}

{% blocktrans %} We process academic information to identify learning requirements, personalise training, assess language development and support teaching. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% blocktrans trimmed %} Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where necessary for individually contracted training, or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for company-funded training under the legitimate interests described above. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} Access to individual academic information is restricted according to the user's role and responsibilities. Learning Needs access is explained in the following section. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Placement tests" %}

{% blocktrans %} The English Grows placement test is available both independently of enrollment and to prospective learners considering an English Course. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} We process your email address, submitted answers and assessment results to calculate a recommended Course level and send you the result. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% blocktrans trimmed %} Legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, in providing the placement assessment you have requested and supporting appropriate Course placement. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} Where you have an assigned teacher, a separate result notification may be sent to that teacher. Otherwise, authorised English Grows staff may receive the result for placement administration. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} The recommendation supports Course placement and does not constitute formal CEFR certification. Completing the test does not subscribe you to marketing. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Service-related communications" %}

{% blocktrans %} We use contact information to send placement results, Course information, scheduling updates and other communications necessary to provide the requested service. Their legal basis follows the corresponding service or processing activity. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Service-related communications are separate from optional marketing messages." %}

{% trans "Optional marketing communications" %}

{% blocktrans %} Where you actively subscribe, we use your email address to send English Grows news, offers and information about our training services. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% blocktrans trimmed %} Your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, together with the applicable requirements of Article 21 LSSI-CE. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} Subscription is optional. You can withdraw consent at any time using the unsubscribe mechanism in our marketing emails, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Enquiries and customer support" %}

{% blocktrans %} We process the information you provide to respond to enquiries and manage communications relating to our services. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% blocktrans trimmed %} Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where you request steps before entering into a contract, or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR where necessary to respond to general enquiries and provide customer support. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Platform operation and security" %}

{% blocktrans %} We process necessary technical information to maintain the website and learning platform, investigate technical problems and protect against unauthorised access. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Legal basis:" %} {% blocktrans trimmed %} Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, based on our legitimate interest in maintaining a secure and functional service, and Article 6(1)(c) where a specific legal obligation applies. {% endblocktrans %}
